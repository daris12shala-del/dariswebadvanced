FRAGRANCE GUIDE

Fragrance Notes

Discover the three layers of a perfume.

01

Top Notes

These are the first scents you notice when you spray a perfume.

Examples: lemon, bergamot and orange.

02

Heart Notes

Heart notes appear after the top notes and form the main character of a perfume.

Examples: rose, jasmine and lavender.

03

Base Notes

Base notes last the longest and give depth to a fragrance.

Examples: vanilla, musk and sandalwood.

How to Choose a Perfume

For everyday wear, fresh and citrus fragrances are a great option. If you prefer something warmer, look for woody, amber and vanilla notes.
