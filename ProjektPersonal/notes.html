Fragrance Notes

Every perfume is created with different layers of scents. These layers are called fragrance notes.

01

Top Notes

Top notes are the first scents you notice when you spray a perfume. They are usually fresh and light.

Examples

Bergamot · Lemon · Orange · Mint

02

Heart Notes

Heart notes appear after the top notes begin to fade. They create the main character of the fragrance.

Examples

Rose · Jasmine · Lavender · Cinnamon

03

Base Notes

Base notes develop later and can remain noticeable for hours. They add warmth and depth to a perfume.

Examples

Vanilla · Oud · Musk · Sandalwood

How Does a Perfume Work?

A perfume changes over time. First you notice the top notes, then the heart notes, and finally the base notes. Together they create a unique fragrance experience.
